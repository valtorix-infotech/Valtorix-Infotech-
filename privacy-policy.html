Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

VALTORIX INFOTECH (“we”, “our”, “us”), based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us or submit a project request, we collect the details you provide:

  • Your name, company name, email address, and phone number
  • Project requirements, timelines, budgets, and preferences
  • Any other information you choose to share in messages or attachments

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and prepare project proposals
  • To provide, manage, and support our services
  • To send a confirmation reply when you submit a form
  • To improve our website and services

We use your details only to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Form submissions are delivered to us by email through our form-delivery provider. We never share your details with third parties for marketing.

5. Embedded content

Our contact page embeds Google Maps to show our location. Google may collect usage data per its own privacy policy when you interact with the map.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to review, correct, or delete the personal information you shared with us. Just contact us using the details below.
