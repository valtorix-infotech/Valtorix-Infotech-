Terms of Service

The simple, transparent terms under which we work with our clients.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our services

VALTORIX INFOTECH provides web development, software solutions, AI & automation, UI/UX design, and related digital services. Every engagement begins with a free consultation followed by a written proposal.

2. Quotes & estimates

Timelines and cost estimates shared in a proposal are based on the requirements discussed. Any change in scope afterwards may revise the timeline and cost, which we will always confirm with you in writing first.

3. Payments

  • Projects typically begin with an agreed advance payment.
  • Remaining payments follow milestones defined in the proposal.
  • All costs are shared transparently — there are no hidden charges.

4. Timelines

We commit to the schedule in your proposal and keep you updated throughout. Timelines may shift if requirements change or if feedback or content from your side is delayed.

5. Revisions

Reasonable revision rounds are included as defined in your proposal, so the final product matches what was agreed.

6. Intellectual property

On full payment, ownership of the final deliverables transfers to you. We may showcase completed work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

7. Support & maintenance

We provide post-launch support as agreed in your proposal. Ongoing maintenance plans are available on request.

8. Confidentiality

Your business information and project details are kept confidential and used only for project discussion and delivery. We never share them with third parties.

9. Limitation of liability

Our liability is limited to the amount paid for the specific project. We are not liable for indirect losses such as lost profits arising from the use of deliverables.
