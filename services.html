01 — Web development

Websites that are fast, findable and sell

A website is judged in the first three seconds on a mid-range Android phone. We design and build for that reality: compressed assets, sensible typography, a server-rendered first paint and zero layout shift.

  • Responsive from 320px up, tested on real devices
  • Technical SEO: clean URLs, sitemaps, schema and metadata
  • WhatsApp and enquiry flows wired to your inbox
  • Analytics and search console set up from day one

03 — AI & automation

Automate the repetitive half of the work

We do not sell “AI” for its own sake. We look for the tasks your team repeats every day — retyping data, answering the same question, compiling a report — and remove them with the simplest thing that works.

  • Support assistants trained on your own content
  • Document extraction: invoices, forms, ID proofs
  • Automated daily and monthly reporting
  • Lead capture, enrichment and follow-up sequences
  • Human-in-the-loop review before anything is published

How we price

What a project tends to cost

These are the ranges we quote against. The final number depends on the feature list, and you get a written estimate before anything starts. Or tell us the problem and we will size it for you.

Band 1

₹10,000 – ₹25,000

A focused build: a marketing site, a small landing page or a single product screen set.

Band 2

₹25,000 – ₹50,000

A small business system: a site with an enquiry flow, or a first version of an internal tool.

Band 3

₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000

A multi-user application with roles, reporting and the integrations that come with them.

Band 4

₹1,00,000 and above

Builds that span several systems, or that need staged delivery across months.

Not sure which band you are in? Send us what you have in mind — we will tell you honestly if the budget does not match the scope, and what a smaller first step would look like.
